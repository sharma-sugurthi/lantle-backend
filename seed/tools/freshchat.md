## What is Freshchat?

Freshchat is the conversational messaging product in the Freshworks suite. It provides a website and in-app chat widget, a team inbox and connections to WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Apple Messages for Business, LINE and SMS, so customers can message on the channel they already use and agents answer in one queue. It is sold on its own, with a free plan for a small number of agents, and it is also bundled into the Freshdesk Omni package for teams that want ticketing, chat and phone from one vendor.

Automation comes in two forms. A no-code bot builder creates rule-based flows for routing, lead capture and FAQ answers. Freddy AI Agent, sold as a metered add-on measured in bot sessions, answers customers autonomously from your knowledge base and connected data, and Freddy Copilot drafts replies, summarises threads and suggests next steps for human agents.

## Where it shines

Channel breadth at a low price is the main draw. Getting WhatsApp Business, Instagram DMs and Apple Messages into a single inbox with assignment rules, canned responses and a mobile app is easy and inexpensive, and the free plan is enough for a couple of agents to run website chat properly. Setup follows the usual Freshworks pattern: click through a wizard, paste a snippet and you are live.

The bot builder is better than average for the segment. Flows can branch on customer attributes, call external APIs to fetch order or account details, and hand to a human with the collected context. For ecommerce teams on Shopify, the native integration surfaces order information in the agent view, and the bot can answer order status questions without a person.

Freddy AI Agent is a credible autonomous layer for common questions. It reads help articles and past resolved conversations, answers in the customer's language and escalates when confidence is low. Copilot is arguably more useful day to day, because summaries and suggested replies speed up every agent rather than only the deflected share.

If you already run Freshdesk or Freshsales, the shared contact records, unified reporting and single admin console make Freshchat the obvious choice over a third-party chat tool, and multilingual support across the widget, articles and bot is thorough.

## Where it falls short

Freshworks packaging is confusing. Freshchat standalone, Freshdesk Omni and the Freddy add-ons overlap and are priced separately, so buyers should insist on a written breakdown before committing. Bot session limits are easy to exhaust on a busy site, and the metered add-on cost is what moves the bill upward over time.

The AI agent is solid but not the category leader. It does not perform multi-step actions across systems the way [Ada](/tools/ada/) does, and its resolution reporting is less specific than what [Intercom](/tools/intercom/) provides for Fin. Teams that want the AI to be the primary support channel should compare those two first.

Deeper reporting, SLAs and advanced routing arrive only on the upper tiers, and at that point the price gap to a full help desk narrows. Very small teams that only need chat and a simple bot may find [Crisp](/tools/crisp/) cheaper and faster to configure.

## Conclusion

Freshchat is a well rounded messaging product for small and mid-sized support teams, and the natural pick for anyone already on Freshworks. It brings WhatsApp, social and in-app chat together at a fair price, the bot builder is practical and Freddy adds useful AI assist. Watch the add-on metering and packaging, and choose a specialist AI agent if autonomous resolution is the main objective.
