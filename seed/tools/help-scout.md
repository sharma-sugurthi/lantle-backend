## What is Help Scout?

Help Scout is a shared inbox built around email conversations rather than tickets. Support mail lands in a mailbox that looks like a normal email client, with assignment, statuses, tags and folders layered on top. Customers never see a ticket number, and replies go out looking like a personal email. Alongside the inbox sit Docs, a hosted knowledge base, and Beacon, an embeddable widget that puts Docs search, a contact form and live chat on your website or inside your app.

The AI features are practical rather than ambitious. AI summarize condenses a long thread before an escalation, AI assist rewrites a draft to be shorter or friendlier, and AI drafts writes a first reply using Docs content and past conversations. AI answers goes a step further and replies to customers directly inside Beacon using your published articles, deflecting simple questions before a person sees them. Pricing moved from a per seat model to one based on the number of contacts you help each month, with a free plan for very small volumes, so you can add as many teammates as you like without the bill moving.

## Where it shines

The inbox is the best part. Collision detection shows who is viewing or replying, notes keep internal discussion inside the thread, and saved replies with merge fields handle repeat questions in a couple of clicks. Workflows automate assignment, tagging and follow-ups with plain conditions, and the whole system can be learned in an afternoon.

Docs is a genuinely good knowledge base for the money. Articles are easy to write and organise, the search is fast, and Beacon surfaces the right article before a customer types a message. Reports show which articles are read and which searches return nothing, which tells you exactly what to write next.

The contact based pricing suits companies where many people touch support part time. Engineers, founders and account managers can all have seats without paying per head, and light users are no longer a budgeting question. Customer profiles pull in data from Shopify, HubSpot, Salesforce and a custom app sidebar, so an agent sees orders or subscription status next to the conversation.

Help Scout also cares about the customer experience on the receiving end. Emails arrive without ticket cruft, satisfaction ratings are collected with a single click in the reply, and the happiness report gives an honest read of how the team is doing.

## Where it falls short

There is no true ticketing model. SLAs, priorities and escalation paths are not first class objects, so teams with contractual response targets end up approximating them with workflows and tags. Reporting is fine for a team lead but shallow for an operations analyst, and custom reports are limited.

Channel coverage is narrower than the omnichannel suites. Chat lives in Beacon, Messenger and Instagram are supported, but there is no native voice and no WhatsApp. Phone heavy teams pair it with a separate telephony tool and lose the single timeline.

The contact based pricing can surprise high volume, low touch teams. A store handling thousands of short conversations a month will pay more than it would on a per seat plan elsewhere. If you need proper tickets, SLAs and deep analytics, [Zendesk](/tools/zendesk/) is the more complete help desk, and [Freshdesk](/tools/freshdesk/) gives you SLAs and round-robin routing at a lower price. Ecommerce brands that live in order data should compare [Gorgias](/tools/gorgias/).

## Conclusion

Help Scout is the right choice for a small or mid sized team that wants support to feel like well organised email rather than a queue of tickets. The inbox, Docs and Beacon fit together well, the AI features remove real minutes from each conversation, and the contact based plans let the whole company take part in support. Teams with SLA commitments, phone as a primary channel or a large analytics appetite should look elsewhere.
