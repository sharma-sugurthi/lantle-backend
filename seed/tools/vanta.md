## What is Vanta?

Vanta is a compliance automation platform that connects to the systems a company already runs and continuously checks them against the controls required by frameworks such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR and PCI DSS. Once linked to a cloud provider, identity provider, source control, HR system and device fleet, it runs automated tests: is multi-factor authentication enforced, are production databases encrypted, do all employees have background checks and security training on file, has every offboarded person had their access removed. Failing tests show up on a dashboard with the owner and the fix.

Around that monitoring core, Vanta provides policy templates that can be edited and published for employee acceptance, a risk register, vendor risk management with automated questionnaire review, access reviews, and a Trust Center page for publishing your security posture to prospects. Auditors work inside the platform, pulling evidence directly rather than requesting screenshots by email, and Vanta maintains a network of audit firms familiar with its exports. An AI layer drafts answers to customer security questionnaires from your existing policies and evidence.

## Where it shines

Time to audit readiness is the main win. A SaaS company with a small engineering team and no security hire can typically get from nothing to a SOC 2 Type 1 audit in a matter of weeks rather than the quarters it used to take with a consultant and a spreadsheet. The integrations do the evidence collection that used to be the bulk of the work, and the tests keep running after the audit so the second year is easier than the first.

The policy library is practical. Templates cover the documents an auditor expects, they are written in plain language, and acceptance tracking means you can prove every employee read them. Combined with the employee onboarding checklist, security training and device agent, Vanta handles the people side of compliance, which is where small companies usually fall down.

Cross framework mapping saves duplicate effort. Controls satisfied for SOC 2 are automatically credited towards ISO 27001 or HIPAA, so adding a second framework later is incremental rather than a fresh project. The Trust Center and questionnaire automation shorten enterprise sales cycles, which is often how the purchase gets justified to the founders.

## Where it falls short

Vanta is priced as an annual contract and the cost climbs as you add frameworks, integrations and employees. For a very early company that has not yet been asked for a SOC 2 report by a customer, it is easy to buy too soon. The audit itself is a separate fee paid to the audit firm.

Automation covers the controls Vanta can test. Anything outside a supported integration, or any custom control, becomes a manual evidence upload with a reminder cadence, and organisations with unusual infrastructure will find more of those than the marketing suggests. Passing Vanta's tests is not the same as being secure, and a green dashboard can breed complacency.

Vanta also does not touch the legal documents that compliance generates. Data processing agreements, vendor contracts and customer security addenda still need to be negotiated, stored and tracked elsewhere. [Ironclad](/tools/ironclad/) handles that side for larger teams, and [DocuSign](/tools/docusign/) covers execution and storage for smaller ones. Policies and questionnaire answers drafted by the platform still need a lawyer or qualified privacy professional to review, particularly for GDPR obligations.

## Conclusion

Vanta is the sensible default for a SaaS company that needs SOC 2, ISO 27001 or GDPR readiness and does not want to hire for it yet. The integrations and continuous tests remove most of the grind, the policy library is usable out of the box, and the audit workflow is well trodden. Buy it when a customer asks for a report, not before, and treat the dashboard as a floor rather than proof of security.
