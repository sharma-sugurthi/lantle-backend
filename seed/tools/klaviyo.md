## What is Klaviyo?

Klaviyo is a marketing automation platform for ecommerce. It connects directly to Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento and most other storefronts and pulls in the full order, browse and cart history of every customer. That data feeds everything else: segments that update in real time, flows triggered by behaviour such as an abandoned checkout or a first purchase, and campaigns sent by email, SMS, push or WhatsApp in supported regions. Predictive fields estimate each customer's next order date, lifetime value and churn risk, and those estimates can be used as segment conditions.

The platform has expanded from a messaging tool into something closer to a customer data platform with messaging on top. Sign up forms, product reviews, a customer service inbox and a data warehouse sync are all part of the product now. Pricing is free up to a small contact list and a monthly send cap, then scales with the number of active profiles, with SMS billed separately by message volume.

## Where it shines

The ecommerce integrations are deep rather than nominal. Because Klaviyo knows what each person bought, viewed and returned, segmentation can be very specific: people who bought a product twice but not in the last ninety days, or visitors who viewed a category three times without buying. Building that segment takes minutes, and it refreshes itself.

Flows are the workhorse. Welcome series, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post purchase, winback and replenishment flows come as pre-built templates that most brands can launch with light editing. The visual builder handles branching on profile data, time delays, A/B splits and channel switching between email and SMS in one flow. For most stores, flows end up producing a large share of attributed revenue with little ongoing effort.

Reporting is tied to actual orders, not opens and clicks. Every campaign and flow shows revenue attributed, and the benchmarks feature compares your metrics with similar brands in your vertical and size band. That makes it easy to spot underperforming flows and justify the spend.

SMS is properly integrated rather than bolted on. Consent collection, quiet hours, two way conversations and the same segmentation engine as email all work together, and the compliance tooling for different countries is handled in the product.

## Where it falls short

Cost rises steeply with list size. Because billing is based on active profiles rather than sends, a store with a large but lightly engaged list pays for contacts it rarely emails. Aggressive list cleaning becomes a financial necessity, and brands often feel the price jump at each tier boundary. SMS costs on top of that can surprise anyone who has not modelled message volume.

The template editor is serviceable but not a designer's tool. Complex layouts often need custom HTML blocks, and mobile rendering needs checking every time. Deliverability is fine when set up correctly, but the platform assumes you know how to warm a sending domain and does not hold your hand.

Klaviyo is built for stores. B2B companies, publishers and service businesses without transaction data lose most of the advantage and will find it expensive for what amounts to newsletters. It also does nothing for acquisition: driving traffic in the first place still depends on search work in [Semrush](/tools/semrush/) and content shaped in [Surfer](/tools/surfer/) or written in [Jasper](/tools/jasper/).

## Conclusion

For an ecommerce brand of almost any size, Klaviyo is the platform to build retention on. The data model, flows and revenue reporting are a clear step above general email tools, and the SMS integration is the best in its class. Go in with a plan for list hygiene and a realistic budget for growth, and if your business does not sell products online, look elsewhere.
