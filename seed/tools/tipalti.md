## What is Tipalti?

Tipalti manages the full payables lifecycle for companies with global supplier bases. It starts with self-service supplier onboarding - vendors register themselves, choose their preferred payment method, and submit required tax documentation through a branded portal. The platform collects and validates W-9s, W-8s, and international equivalents automatically, reducing the compliance burden on the finance team.

From there, Tipalti handles invoice capture and data extraction, GL coding suggestions, multi-level approval workflows, and payment execution in over 190 countries and 120 currencies. Payments can go out via ACH, wire, PayPal, prepaid debit, or local payment rails depending on the supplier's country and preference. Once payments are made, the platform reconciles them back to the ERP - supporting NetSuite, Sage Intacct, QuickBooks, Microsoft Dynamics, SAP and others.

The platform was built for scale. Companies paying hundreds or thousands of suppliers, affiliates, freelancers or marketplace sellers each month are the core audience. The entire flow - from "new vendor signs up" through "payment hits their account and the ERP is updated" - can run with minimal manual intervention once configured.

## Where it shines

Tax compliance automation is a genuine differentiator. Collecting the right forms from every supplier, validating them, and handling withholding calculations across jurisdictions is tedious, error-prone work that most AP teams dread. Tipalti moves that burden to the system. Suppliers provide their own details, the platform validates and stores the documentation, and the finance team gets a clean audit trail without chasing paperwork.

The payment execution engine handles cross-border complexity that simpler tools cannot. Currency conversion, local banking rails, sanctions screening, and fraud detection are built in rather than bolted on. For a company paying creators in thirty countries, this removes an enormous operational headache.

Controls and audit trail quality are strong enough to satisfy external auditors and SOX compliance requirements. Every approval, payment instruction, and status change is logged with timestamps and user attribution, which simplifies audit prep considerably.

Supplier self-service reduces the back-and-forth that typically clogs AP inboxes. Instead of emailing forms and chasing missing bank details, vendors handle their own onboarding and payment method updates through the portal.

## Where it falls short

Implementation is a project, not an afternoon. Tipalti serves mid-market and enterprise customers, and the setup reflects that - expect weeks of configuration, testing and training before go-live. Companies looking for something they can spin up over a weekend will find the onboarding process heavy.

For businesses with only a few dozen domestic vendors, Tipalti is overkill. The platform's value scales with the number of suppliers, the geographic spread of payments, and the complexity of tax compliance requirements. A small US company paying twenty domestic vendors would be better served by [BILL](/tools/bill/), which handles straightforward AP workflows at a lower price point and with faster setup.

Pricing is enterprise-grade and not published. Expect a sales conversation and a proposal tailored to volume and feature requirements.

For teams whose primary need is invoice collaboration and coding rather than global payment execution, [Stampli](/tools/stampli/) offers a lighter-weight approach that layers on top of existing ERPs without the implementation overhead.

## Conclusion

Tipalti is the tool to reach for when payables complexity goes global. The combination of supplier onboarding, tax compliance, multi-currency payments and ERP reconciliation in a single platform is hard to replicate with point solutions. The implementation investment is real, but for companies at scale, the operational savings and compliance confidence justify it.
