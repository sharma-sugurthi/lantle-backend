## What is Jotform Sign?

Jotform Sign is the e-signature product from Jotform, the online form builder. Rather than a standalone signing tool, it is a document type inside the Jotform workspace. You upload a PDF or start from one of the hundreds of signable templates, drag signature, initials, date and text fields onto it in the same builder used for forms, assign fields to signer roles, and send. Signers complete the document from an email link or a shared URL, and the signed copy is stored in Jotform with an audit trail. Signatures are enforceable under ESIGN and UETA, Jotform holds SOC 2, and HIPAA features are available on the higher plans.

The distinctive part is what happens around the signature. Because a Jotform Sign document is a Jotform submission, everything that works with forms works with signed documents. Approval flows route a signed document to a manager for sign off. Conditional logic shows or hides fields. Tables collect submissions for reporting. The workflow builder can send a document for signature after a form is filled in, or notify a Slack channel or create a Google Sheets row when signing completes. Signed data can be pushed to Salesforce, HubSpot, Dropbox, Google Drive and the other integrations Jotform already supports.

Pricing follows Jotform's usual model. The free plan includes a small number of signed documents per month alongside the form limits, and paid plans raise the caps for signed documents, submissions and storage rather than charging per seat.

## Where it shines

Teams already using Jotform get e-signature without a new tool, a new login or a new vendor contract. A clinic that collects intake through Jotform can add a consent signature to the same flow, a property manager can move from an application form to a lease, and an HR team can chain an offer letter to onboarding forms.

The template library is large and practical, covering leases, NDAs, consent forms, waivers, employment agreements and invoices, and the builder is quick to learn. Field placement on an uploaded PDF works well, and the ability to include a signature field inside a regular form, so the signer fills in the details and signs in one pass, is something dedicated signature tools do not offer.

Automation and approval flows are more capable than most signature tools at this price. Multi step routing, conditional branching and integrations to Slack, Google Sheets, Airtable and Zapier are included on the paid plans rather than gated to enterprise.

## Where it falls short

Jotform Sign is not built for contract heavy workflows. There is no bulk send comparable to the market leaders, signer identity verification is limited to basic options, and a lawyer negotiating an agreement will find no redlining, clause library or review tools. It formats and routes documents; it does not assess them. For high value agreements with large counterparties, [DocuSign](/tools/docusign/) remains the safer and more recognised choice, and [Adobe Acrobat Sign](/tools/adobe-acrobat-sign/) offers deeper compliance options for regulated industries.

The signing limits on the free and lower plans are tight, and because the caps are shared with form submissions and storage, a busy account can hit a limit unexpectedly. Enterprise features such as SSO and custom domains sit on the Enterprise plan, which is quoted on request.

The product also lacks CRM depth. Integrations push data out, but generating a document from a Salesforce or HubSpot record with fields prefilled is not as developed as [PandaDoc](/tools/pandadoc/) offers.

## Conclusion

Jotform Sign makes sense for organisations already running forms in Jotform that want signatures on those same documents, and for small businesses whose signing needs are consent forms, waivers and simple agreements rather than negotiated contracts. The approval flows and automation are its edge. Anyone signing complex or high value agreements at volume should treat it as a convenience feature and use a dedicated e-signature platform.
