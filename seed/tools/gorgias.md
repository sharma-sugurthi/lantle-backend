## What is Gorgias?

Gorgias is a help desk built specifically for online stores. It connects directly to Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento and WooCommerce, pulls the customer's order history into the ticket sidebar, and lets an agent refund, cancel, duplicate or edit an order without leaving the conversation. Email, live chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram all land in one queue, and a voice add-on brings phone calls into the same timeline. Pricing is per month by ticket volume rather than per agent, which suits stores with a handful of people handling a lot of conversations.

The headline feature is AI Agent, an autonomous assistant that answers customer emails and chats using your help centre, store policies and order data. It can look up shipping status, apply a discount code or start a return through a connected returns app, and it hands over to a person when it is not confident. The company also sells Convert, a set of on-site chat campaigns and product recommendations aimed at turning support into revenue, which reflects how Gorgias thinks about the category: support as a sales channel rather than a cost centre.

## Where it shines

The Shopify integration is the deepest of any help desk. Order data is live, actions are two way, and macros can include variables such as tracking numbers so a canned reply about a delayed parcel is complete on the first send. Rules trigger on intent and sentiment detection, which means an angry message about a missing order can be routed to a senior agent while a simple where is my order question gets an automatic answer.

AI Agent works well for the repetitive questions that dominate a store's inbox. Because it can read the order and take action, it resolves cases that a knowledge base bot can only explain. Stores commonly see a large share of tickets handled without a person during peak periods, and the per resolution pricing means the cost scales with what it actually does.

The ecommerce app ecosystem is broad. Klaviyo, Recharge, Yotpo, Aftership, Loop Returns and Attentive all have native integrations, and the customer sidebar composes them into a single profile. A subscription problem, a review and a delivery exception are all visible in one place.

Reporting is oriented around what a merchant cares about: tickets by channel and intent, revenue influenced by support, and agent performance during a sale. Chat flows and self-service order tracking in the chat widget deflect the most common questions before a ticket is created.

## Where it falls short

Gorgias is only sensible if you run a store on a supported platform. Software companies, agencies and B2B teams get a product full of features they cannot use and miss the custom objects and ticket forms a general help desk provides. Custom platforms need integration work to reach the same depth.

Ticket based pricing punishes growth. A viral product launch or a busy holiday season can push you into the next tier or into overage charges, and AI Agent resolutions are billed separately. The entry tiers are also thin on automation and reporting, so the price a growing brand actually pays sits well above the entry figure.

Voice is an add-on rather than a strength, and reporting is less flexible than Zendesk Explore. Multi brand setups with different policies per store can get awkward. For a general purpose help desk with deeper ticketing, compare [Zendesk](/tools/zendesk/), and for a cheaper store friendly option with a free plan, look at [Freshdesk](/tools/freshdesk/) or [HubSpot Service Hub](/tools/hubspot-service-hub/).

## Conclusion

Gorgias is the best help desk for a direct to consumer brand on Shopify or BigCommerce. Nothing else lets agents and the AI act on orders as directly, and the revenue focused features match how ecommerce teams are measured. Outside of ecommerce it is the wrong tool, and inside it the ticket based pricing deserves a careful forecast before you commit.
