## What is SignNow?

SignNow is the e-signature product from airSlate, a company that also sells pdfFiller and a business workflow automation product under the airSlate name. It covers the standard signing workflow: upload a document, place fields, assign roles, set the signing order and send. Recipients sign from any browser or the mobile app, and the completed document includes a court admissible audit trail. Signatures comply with ESIGN, UETA and eIDAS, and the platform holds SOC 2, HIPAA and GDPR compliance.

The feature list is broader than the price suggests. Templates, document groups that bundle several files into a single signing session, bulk send, conditional fields that show or hide based on earlier answers, calculated fields and signing links that can be embedded on a website are all present on the business tiers. Payment collection through Stripe or Authorize.net can be added to a document, so a signed agreement and a deposit happen in one step. Teams share templates and documents through shared folders with admin controls.

Integrations cover the mainstream systems: Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft 365 including Teams and Dynamics, Google Workspace, Box, Dropbox, NetSuite and Oracle. The API is well documented, with a free sandbox, SDKs and embedded signing, and airSlate sells API plans separately for product teams. Because SignNow sits inside the airSlate portfolio, customers who need multi step document automation can move up to the workflow product without changing vendors.

## Where it shines

Value is the headline. SignNow includes capabilities on its mid tier plans that competitors reserve for their top plans, including conditional logic, document groups, signing links and payment collection. A small business that would hit the envelope limits or feature gates of DocuSign's cheaper tiers can usually get everything it needs from SignNow at a lower per user cost, and the plans do not cap the number of documents sent.

The mobile apps are good. Field placement, sending and in person signing on a tablet all work well, which matters for field sales, property management and healthcare intake, where the signer is standing in front of you.

Document groups solve a real annoyance. When a new hire needs to sign five forms, or a tenant needs a lease plus three addenda, SignNow sends them as one package with one link and one signing session, and the audit trail covers the group.

## Where it falls short

Brand recognition is lower than the market leader, and some enterprise counterparties are more comfortable receiving a [DocuSign](/tools/docusign/) envelope than a SignNow link. That is a perception issue rather than a legal one, but it influences procurement teams.

The interface is functional rather than polished and has inconsistencies between the web app and the mobile apps. Advanced administration such as SSO, custom branding and detailed audit reporting requires the top tier or enterprise packaging. There is no free plan, only a short trial, so occasional users cannot sit on a free tier the way they can with DocuSign or [Dropbox Sign](/tools/dropbox-sign/).

SignNow is a signature and simple form tool. There is no proposal builder, pricing table or content library, and no AI assisted review of agreement terms, so a team that wants document generation should look at [PandaDoc](/tools/pandadoc/). As with every product in this category, it records that a document was signed; it does not tell you whether the document should have been.

## Conclusion

SignNow is the best value in mainstream e-signature. It covers nearly everything a small or mid size business needs for signing, including conditional fields, document groups and payments, at a lower cost than the better known names, and the mobile experience is a strength. The trade offs are a plainer interface, less name recognition and no free plan. For a team that is paying for signatures and finds the leader too expensive, it should be the first alternative evaluated.
